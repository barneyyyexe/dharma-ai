# Dharma AI

> **Our past × Our dilemmas × Modern AI**

Dharma AI is an experimental AI project built around the **Mahabharata** — a foundational part of India's civilizational heritage and a vast body of human experiences, dilemmas, relationships, choices, conflicts, responsibilities, and consequences preserved through generations.

The goal is **not** to build another Mahabharata question-answering bot.

Instead, Dharma AI explores whether the knowledge, experiences, philosophical ideas, and human conflicts preserved in our past can help people think more clearly about the problems they face today.

The Mahabharata should mostly work **behind the scenes**.

A user should be able to simply say:

> *"Someone I trusted betrayed me. I'm angry and I want revenge, but I don't know if that's the right thing to do."*

The system should understand the human conflict behind those words, find relevant situations and ideas from the Mahabharata, examine their context and consequences, and then help the person look at their own situation from a different perspective.

It should feel like a thoughtful conversation — **not a lecture, sermon, or mythology search engine.**

---

## The Idea

Human beings have faced difficult choices for thousands of years.

The circumstances change.

Technology changes.

Society changes.

But many of the underlying human conflicts remain surprisingly familiar:

- Anger
- Betrayal
- Revenge
- Loyalty
- Love
- Family
- Duty
- Ambition
- Fear
- Sacrifice
- Power
- Responsibility
- Forgiveness
- Justice
- Attachment
- Conflict between personal desire and responsibility

The Mahabharata contains an enormous range of such human situations.

Dharma AI attempts to connect that inherited knowledge with the dilemmas of modern life.
